### UTP

<br/>

[![qAjIfyb.md.png](https://iili.io/qAjIfyb.md.png)](https://freeimage.host/i/qAjIfyb)

<br/>
UTP stands for **Unshielded Twisted Pair** cable . Unshielded means it has no  metallic shield making it vulnerable to electrical interference 


There are total 4 pairs of wires paired together ie total 8 wires


The twist in cables help to protect against electro magnetic interference (EMI)


No  Cables  in UTP ethernet


- 10 BASE-T and 100 BASE-T have 2 pairs (4 wires)
- 1000 BASE-T and 10GBASE-T have 4 pairs (8 wires)
