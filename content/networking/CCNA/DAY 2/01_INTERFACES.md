Interfaces are ports to connect network devices like switches ,end hosts and routers . Each device has its own interfaces


These interfaces are all RJ45 ports (RJ= Registered Jack)


## RJ45

    The RJ45 connectors are used on end of copper ethernet cables . All ethernet cables are not copper cables

## ETHERNET

    Ethernet is collection of network protocols and standards

###  Why do we neeed Network Protocols

    Network Protocols are set of instruction to carry out communication between networks
