### Installation For Arch2 Linux

1. Download Cisco Packet Tracer from the [official Cisco website](https://www.netacad.com/cisco-packet-tracer) or [My Drive](https://drive.proton.me/urls/KGA98TKYP4#sitWOO8ikXBP)   
2. Follow the installation wizard for your operating system
3. Launch the application and log in with your Cisco NetAcad credentials

### Initial Configuration

- Set your preferences in **Edit > Preferences**
- Familiarize yourself with the main interface
- Explore the device palette on the left sidebar---
